# Reflection after failed book architectures

The complete-book WordFrontier quality round passes all twelve fresh decodes
and loses every book against the ordinary pinned bzip3 archive. The separate
fixed-word-class, phrase-template, word-order, byte PPM, global bit mixture
and conditional mixer screens also lack headroom. Source-only entropy proxies
are diagnostics and are not complete frames. The new source editions and
the baseline adapter were independently checked; dropping books or languages
would hide the failure.

The registered complete-book result is:

| Work | WordFrontier complete bytes | bzip3 complete bytes | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Pride and Prejudice | 169,043 | 161,119 | +4.92% |
| War and Peace | 758,018 | 732,272 | +3.52% |
| Don Quijote | 534,093 | 500,612 | +6.69% |
| Madame Bovary | 193,916 | 179,597 | +7.97% |
| Die Verwandlung | 38,277 | 35,251 | +8.58% |
| こころ | 101,920 | 97,505 | +4.53% |

These results do not support a book-compression win. Historical dictionary
and page-access improvements address other scorecards and do not offset them.

The class-factorization screen identifies lost word identity and paid
within-class ranks as large costs. Its fixed word-run tokenizer also treats
Japanese passages as rare, long tokens. More class-history rows increase the
model bill faster than they reduce the event stream. The phrase-skeleton
proxies have too few profitable whole-word constructions after event/operand
prices. Those tests reject their specific fixed representations.

The causal bit mixture reveals a different failure. Conditional logit mixing
beats a global mixer on five of six prefixes, but still exceeds bzip3 before
framing. Its bounded maps replace contexts millions of times, and separate
previous-word and current-prefix experts do not learn a joint next-word spelling
prediction. Its APM worsens all six; increased probability precision did not
close the earlier global-mixture gap. These observations do not rule out PAQ,
neural prediction or all word models.

The next falsifier therefore changes causal state representation and the
spelling condition: exact bounded previous-token/current-prefix joint keys,
packed retained bit-prefix states, all byte orders one through eight, and
Unicode scalar/script segmentation as a fixed CJK ablation. The table and
arithmetic contract must be pinned before scoring. Its fixed ablations must
report losses, replacement counts and memory together. A native frame follows
only if measured headroom can pay framing and is meaningful relative to the
35% whole-book aspiration. Merely removing probability quantization or a few
operand bytes cannot supply the required effect size.

A separately pinned small-memory neural text compressor is an empirical
research control. Its actual archives, failures and fresh decodes will test
how far the new predictors lag a mature adaptive implementation. It cannot
be presented as a newly invented algorithm. The reserved books remain unopened
until a frozen qualifying candidate is available.

The registered joint-token/prefix follow-up is now closed: retained-prefix
packing reduces churn and joint word conditioning helps its own baseline, but
all six ideal sizes still exceed bzip3. It is worse than the earlier conditional
mixer on five cases. The attribution credits only roughly 5–8 KiB to the word
experts, far short of the additional 35% target reduction. This is a failure of
the fixed representation, not evidence that all contextual word models fail.

The clause-edit model also loses every case after donor, alignment and route
costs. A single semantic boundary correction keeps hardwrapped single newlines
inside sentences. It improves the free-information residual proxy on five
Latin-script cases by 6.8–16.7%, but the paid format still loses all six and
the Japanese proxy loses too. Free donor/operation information is not an
achievable archive or a universal bound. Removing the word grammar's 64 KiB
payload resets similarly saves only 834 bytes across the six fixed prefixes.

The next finite diagnostic changes the lexical probability factorization:
one joint word-and-separator stream, variable-order suffix contexts with
complete successor distributions, explicit exclusion or justified discounting,
and a paid terminated spelling model. The earlier word-cache test capped each
successor list at 16 and priced spelling with an order-one model; merely adding
rows would preserve those limitations. The new protocol must fix tokenization,
memory, updates and six development sources before any outcomes. Separate
spelling, separator and transition costs will determine whether a native wire
is justified. The mature external PAQ reference supplies a modeling calibration,
not credit for an invention or a new corpus outcome.

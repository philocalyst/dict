printed its complete output through `roundtrip ok`. The wrapper then attempted
to assign the process status to zsh's read-only `status` parameter and exited.
Consequently that one runner exit status is unavailable; it is not represented
as zero. The completed process was not rerun. A corrected wrapper continued
only samples 2 through 18, all of which returned status zero. The failed
bookkeeping command emitted: `zsh:1: read-only variable: status`.
